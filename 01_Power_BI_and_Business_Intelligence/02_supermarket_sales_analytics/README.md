# 🛒 Supermarket Retail Sales Analytics Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
An executive Power BI retail analytics dashboard analyzing transactional sales data across supermarket branches, product categories, customer demographics, payment methods, and profit margins.

---

## 📊 Key Analytical Dimensions & KPIs

### 1. **Revenue & Financial Performance**
- **Total Gross Revenue & Profit**: Total sales volume, gross margin percentage ($$), and cost of goods sold (COGS).
- **Branch Comparisons**: Comparative sales benchmarks across multiple store locations and regional clusters.

### 2. **Product Line & Inventory Analytics**
- **Top Product Categories**: Electronic accessories, fashion, food & beverages, health & beauty, and home lifestyle.
- **Unit Price vs Volume**: Quantity sold per transaction and average transaction value (ATV).

### 3. **Customer Demographics & Payment Behavior**
- **Customer Segmentation**: Member vs Normal customer purchasing habits.
- **Payment Method Distribution**: E-wallet, Cash, and Credit Card utilization.
- **Customer Ratings & Satisfaction**: Average customer feedback ratings categorized by product lines.

---

## 📁 Files Included
- `supermarket_sales_analysis.pbix`: Full interactive Microsoft Power BI Report File with DAX measures and custom visuals.
- `supermarket_sales_data.csv`: Transactional dataset for data modeling and exploration.
