# 💳 Credit Card Financial & Transaction Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
A comprehensive banking business intelligence dashboard suite analyzing credit card operations, revenue streams, customer segmentation, transaction channels, and credit risk factors.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Modules & Key Reports

### 1. **Credit Card Transaction Report** (`credit_card_transaction_report.pdf`)
- **Financial KPIs**:
  - **Total Revenue**: Total merchant interchange fees, interest earned, and annual fees.
  - **Total Transaction Amount & Volume**: Quarterly progression and month-over-month volume shifts.
  - **Interest & Fee Analysis**: Total interest accrued vs total transaction value.
- **Card Category Performance**:
  - Breakdown across **Blue**, **Silver**, **Gold**, and **Platinum** card tiers.
  - Transaction method shares (**Swipe**, **Chip / Contactless**, **Online eCommerce**).
- **Expenditure Categories**:
  - Bills, Entertainment, Fuel, Grocery, Travel, and Personal Care spend distributions.

### 2. **Credit Card Customer Analytics Report** (`credit_card_customer_report.pdf`)
- **Customer Segmentation**:
  - Revenue distribution across **Age Brackets** (20-30, 30-40, 40-50, 50-60, 60+).
  - Income Groups (High, Medium, Low) and Educational attainment levels (Graduate, High School, Post-Graduate, Doctorate).
  - Top Job Classifications (Businessmen, White Collar, Self-Employed, Blue Collar, Retirees).
- **Risk & Delinquency Metrics**:
  - Delinquency rate percentage tracking.
  - Average Credit Limit Utilization Ratio across customer profiles.
  - Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT).
